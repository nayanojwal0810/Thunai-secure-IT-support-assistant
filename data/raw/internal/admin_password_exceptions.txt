### Policy Statement
This policy governs password exception handling for privileged administrative accounts within Thunai Demo Company systems and infrastructure.

### Privileged Account Standards
All privileged accounts must use passphrases of at least 16 characters in length. In addition, hardware-backed MFA is required for every administrative sign-in event. Shared administrator passwords are prohibited across all infrastructure environments.

### Exception Governance and Approvals
An exception to standard privileged password requirements must receive written Security Lead approval prior to activation. Ordinary IT support staff cannot approve privileged password exceptions. Every granted exception must be recorded with a specific business reason and a definite expiration date in the tracking system.

### Exception Duration and Closure
Password exceptions expire after seven calendar days. Administrators are responsible for tracking active deviations. Once the time limit elapses, expired exceptions are reviewed and closed to ensure the security baseline is restored.
