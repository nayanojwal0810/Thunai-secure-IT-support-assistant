When to page
If you have already determined that a page is needed, go straight to Paging Customer Support Systems.
This page explains when you should (and should not) page the Customer Support Systems team. Paging interrupts whoever is on-call, so it should be reserved for situations that genuinely cannot wait until the next CSS team member comes online.
For how Customer Support Systems works an incident once it is raised, see Incidents.
Paging is based on impact
We page based on impact, not on whether a component has failed. Something can be broken and still not warrant a page if the impact is minimal.
When deciding, ask yourself one question:
Can we still service our customers?
If the answer is “no” (or “not for much longer”), and there is no viable workaround, it is likely page-worthy. The criticality level of the affected system will also help you gauge how serious the impact is.
Page Customer Support Systems when
- A system we manage is down or broken, it is having a material impact on customers, and there is no viable short-term workaround.
  - Example: a Zendesk Global or Zendesk US Government outage or major degradation.
- A failure prevents a significant portion of Support from working tickets or performing key customer workflows, even if customers are not yet aware of it.
  - Example: tickets are not being processed by the Ticket Processor.
  - Example: the Salesforce to Zendesk sync is not working.
  - Example: the Support Ops Super App or Salesforce integration is failing and Support cannot perform key customer account or ticket workflows.
- An automation or integration we own is actively causing harm. This includes sending incorrect or duplicate communications to customers, modifying or deleting data incorrectly, or exposing data to the wrong party.
  - Example: a trigger or automation is mass-emailing customers incorrectly.
  - Example: an AI agent integration is responding to customers inappropriately.
- There is a suspected security or compliance issue involving our systems.
  - Example: tokens or credentials are suspected to be compromised.
- A declared GitLab incident requires Customer Support Systems involvement to be resolved.
  - Example: Support needs urgent changes to Zendesk (such as temporary triggers, bulk ticket actions, or form changes) to manage the customer impact of an incident.
Do not page Customer Support Systems when
- A reasonable workaround exists and the impact is tolerable until the next CSS team member comes online.
- The issue is cosmetic, or affects a single user or a small number of users.
  - Example: one agent cannot see a view, or a macro contains a typo.
- It only affects reporting or dashboards.
  - Example: a Zendesk Explore report or dashboard is broken or showing incorrect data.
- It is a request for a change or new functionality.
  - Example: Support Portal changes, new macros, or changes to existing triggers.
Examples
The table below gives some common scenarios. These are guidance, not a complete list; always fall back to the impact question.
| Scenario | Page? | Why | 
|---|---|---|
| Zendesk Global or Zendesk US Government is down | Yes | Customers cannot be serviced | 
| Ticket Processor is not processing tickets | Yes | Breaks key Support workflows | 
| Salesforce to Zendesk sync is failing | Yes | Breaks key Support workflows | 
| Super App or Salesforce integration is failing and blocking account or ticket workflows | Yes | Breaks key Support workflows | 
| An automation is sending duplicate emails to customers | Yes | Actively causing harm to customers | 
| An Explore dashboard is not loading | No | Reporting only | 
| A single agent has a permissions issue | No | Affects a single user | 
| A request to change the Support Portal | No | Feature request, not an incident | 
When you are unsure
If you are not sure whether something meets the criteria above, raise it in the #customer_support_systems Slack channel first.
If it is not acknowledged within 30 minutes and the impact is growing, page Customer Support Systems.
Customer Support Systems does not currently provide 24/7 coverage in Slack. Outside of APAC/AMER working hours, if the issue meets the criteria to page Customer Support Systems, page rather than waiting on a Slack response.
