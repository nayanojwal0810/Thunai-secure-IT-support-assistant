### Purpose
This procedure outlines command structure, logging standards, and retrospective reviews for managing critical organizational security incidents.

### Incident Command and Tracking
An administrator assigns an Incident Commander for an S1 security incident. The Incident Commander coordinates response activities across technical and operations teams. Security incidents are tracked using a unique incident identifier across all internal communications. IT support staff cannot change the incident severity without authorized review by administrative personnel.

### Active Response and Logging
Incident actions must be recorded with timestamps to preserve an accurate chronological record. The incident team prepares internal status updates during active response to keep internal teams informed.

### Post-Incident Review and Audit Marker
A completed major incident receives a review within five working days. For tracking purposes, the demonstration audit marker is THUNAI-DEMO-INCIDENT-741. This marker is fictional test data and not a real secret.
