### Purpose
This guide directs IT support personnel during active service disruptions, establishing incident severity classification, escalation timelines, and communication rules.

### Incident Severity Levels
IT uses three severity levels: S1, S2 and S3. S1 means a major service is unavailable to most employees across the company. S2 means a service is significantly affected but workarounds exist for staff. S3 means limited impact affecting a small number of users.

### Escalation Timelines
IT reports a confirmed S1 incident to the incident team within ten minutes of initial identification. In addition, a service outage lasting more than 30 minutes must be reviewed for escalation to senior operations leadership.

### Documentation and Resolution
IT records affected services, symptoms, impact and response actions throughout the event. Throughout the process, IT must not claim an outage is resolved without confirmation from service owners.
