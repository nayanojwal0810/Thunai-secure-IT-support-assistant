### Policy Statement
This policy defines governance controls, regional gateway locations, and authorization requirements for administrative network access across corporate infrastructure.

### Gateway Availability and Access Controls
Administrative network gateways are available in fictional Pune and Hyderabad locations. Only approved administrators may request administrative gateway access to perform management tasks. In all cases, gateway access requires hardware-backed MFA before authentication is granted. IT support staff cannot approve privileged gateway access under any circumstances.

### Emergency Access Governance
In urgent operational conditions, temporary emergency gateway access expires after 30 minutes. Requesting emergency access requires a recorded incident reference in the ticket registry before privileges are provisioned. In addition, every privileged gateway session must be logged continuously to track administrative activity.

### Supervisory Oversight
The Security Lead reviews emergency access usage to confirm proper operational justification and adherence to organizational security policies.
